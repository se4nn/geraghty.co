<Section title="Steeped, Not Brewed">
<Column>

It turns out tea isn’t brewed, as I’d always thought. It’s steeped. Brewing puts the heat in direct contact with the water, while steeping just soaks the leaves until their flavour comes out.

</Column>
<Column>

So the brand draws out what’s inside too. Each range leads with one bold colour that tells you what’s in the box before you’ve read a word, so you know whether you’re reaching for morning or night.

</Column>
</Section>

<Crop window={[1416, 812]} src={packsFlat} rect={[-127.5, -151, 1671, 1114]} alt="The Unwind and Rise packs laid flat on cream: a black grid over a gradient, pale lilac deepening to violet for Unwind, pale yellow deepening to acid yellow for Rise, each with the range and the tea’s name in a black tab up the side" />

<Section title="Rise and Unwind">
<Column>

The two ranges do opposite jobs. They had to be different enough to tell apart, and close enough to read as one club. So I gave both the same pack: a grid with a gradient running down it, for the hours passing through the day.

</Column>
<Column>

The colour is the difference. Rise is acid yellow, a nod to the morning sun, and Unwind is purple, for the night drawing in. Everything else stays the same, down to the black tab the tea’s name sits in.

</Column>
</Section>

<Section title="Join the Partea">
<Column>

The type is a heavy condensed sans in capitals, set big enough to fill the side of a box. On the shipping boxes, “Join the Partea” repeats until it runs off every edge. On the packs it turns on its side.

</Column>
<Column>

The rest of the copy is the same kind of joke: “Smells like tea spirit” on one poster, “Let the loose-leaf tea loose” on another, and “Brew can do it” along the packing tape.

</Column>
</Section>

<Section title="Brew Can Do It">
<Column>

The box is the first thing a subscriber sees each month, so it carries the brand on its own: plain kraft card, “Your monthly tea subscription” in black, and a band of green tape across the top, the one colour that belongs to neither range.

</Column>
<Column>

From the tin to the tape, it’s the same few parts: one grid, one typeface, two gradients and a pun.

</Column>
</Section>
