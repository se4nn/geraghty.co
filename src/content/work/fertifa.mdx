<Section title="A Blank Slate">
<Column>

Fertifa came to us with a name and not much else. Their customers were founders, People teams and benefits leads at startups, tech companies and every kind of employer in between. The people actually using it were their teams.

</Column>
<Column>

That meant designing for two very different moments. Whatever we made had to be professional enough for a benefits review, and gentle enough for someone reading it alone, late at night.

</Column>
</Section>

<Section title="Three Pillars">
<Column>

Over four or five rounds of workshops and feedback with Eileen, Fertifa’s CEO, and Roxy, their Head of Marketing, we worked out what Fertifa actually stood for. It came down to three pillars.

</Column>
<Column>

**Outcome:**

The pillars didn’t stay in a deck. “From start to finish and beyond” and “Supporting biological differences” went straight onto the posters.

</Column>
</Section>

<Section title="Two Routes">
<Column>

We presented two directions. The first, *Facing reproductive health issues together*, put people’s own worries in the headline and set faces behind reeded glass. The second, *Supporting people through their reproductive healthcare challenges*, was softer: a leaf mark, lemon and sloe, and portraits in arches.

</Column>
<Column>

**Outcome:**

Fertifa chose the first, and we brought the warmth of the second along with it. The glass went after their feedback. It hinted there was something to hide, the opposite of what we wanted to say.

</Column>
</Section>

<Section title="The Mark">
<Column>

The logomark is three ideas in one shape: the F of Fertifa, a + for health and a new shoot for life.

</Column>
<Column>

On its own it’s a stamp for app icons, letters and the corner of a poster. On the end of the wordmark it reads as a quiet plus.

</Column>
</Section>

<Section title="One Colour, Not Ten">
<Column>

In 2022 every startup was reaching for bright, loud colour (think Deliveroo and Monzo). A brand about something this personal needed to stay calm.

</Column>
<Column>

So one colour does the heavy lifting: a bold violet the team nicknamed “Blurple”, with light tints for backgrounds and deep shades for type.

</Column>
</Section>

<Section title="Saying It Out Loud">
<Column>

The tone of voice says what people are actually thinking, in their words rather than ours: “I want to know more about IVF options.” “Reproductive challenges can be daunting, we’re here to change that.”

</Column>
<Column>

The illustrations do the rest. People drawn in a single purple line rather than stock photos of clinics, so the subject feels human without getting too close.

</Column>
</Section>

All of it went into a 42-page brand guide covering logo, type, colour, photography, illustration, advertising, social, stationery and the website’s design system.

</Column>

**Outcome:**

The whole brand, guide and all, landed in time for Fertifa’s relaunch.

</Column>
